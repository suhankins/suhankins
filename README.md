## My most 🌸kawaii🌸 projects

* **[Murazu 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4543000/Murazu_2/)** - solo developed platformer where you launch yourself like a rocket to speedrun your way through an abandoned facility (\*^▽^\*)
* **[Kerotan and GA-KO Checker Δ](https://github.com/suhankins/delta-kerotan-and-gako-checker)** - Website that reads your save file in [Metal Gear Solid Δ: Snake Eater](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2417610/METAL_GEAR_SOLID_D_SNAKE_EATER/) and tells you which collectables are you missing and where.
* **[Ducklake](https://github.com/suhankins/ducklake)** - web toy with a lake full of ducks you can feed, controlled by a simple entity system with physics and state machine AI 🦆
* **[RAID: WWII Files](https://github.com/suhankins/raid-ww2-files)** - View your statistics in [RAID: World War II](https://store.steampowered.com/app/414740/RAID_World_War_II/) and check calendar of all Daily Bounties.
